概述

《腕上天文台》是一款运行于 HarmonyOS 手表的天文科普与天体模型应用。我们遵循最小必要原则：天体模型、姿态跟随和界面效果均在设备本地完成；应用不建立用户账号，不收集、出售或上传用户个人信息。

权限与传感器说明

  • 旋转矢量与陀螺仪：在天体模型的跟随模式中，根据手腕姿态旋转天体与星空背景。数据仅在模型页实时处理，离开页面后停止订阅，不上传、不持久保存。
  • 质感窗压力传感器：在支持该能力的华为设备上，根据质感窗压力生成天体光照阴影。普通触屏按压不会用于此功能；压力值仅在本地实时处理。
  • 振动：用于表冠翻页、模型缩放、边界提示和长按确认的触感反馈。
  • 网络与网络状态：仅在用户主动查看隐私政策、检查更新或使用明确标注的网络功能时连接网络。

应用不申请定位、相机、麦克风、通讯录、短信、通话记录或健康数据权限。

数据处理方式

  • 传感器数据只在运行内存中短暂使用，离开对应页面后立即停止处理。
  • 灵敏度、浏览方式、刻度及显示开关等偏好仅保存在应用私有目录，不包含身份信息。
  • 设置偏好保留至用户恢复默认、清除应用数据或卸载应用。
  • 当前版本不集成广告、行为统计、账号登录或个性化推荐 SDK，不向第三方出售、出租或共享个人信息。
  • 用户打开本页时，网页托管服务可能按互联网连接的通常方式接收 IP 地址及浏览器基本请求信息；应用开发者不据此建立用户档案。

第三方服务

应用不包含广告、分析或社交分享服务。隐私政策网页由 GitHub Pages 托管；其网络日志处理受 GitHub 自身政策约束。天体图像、纹理与模型素材的许可和来源说明，不代表素材提供者能够取得用户数据。

Privacy Policy

Wrist Observatory · Effective and updated: October 4, 2026

Overview

Wrist Observatory is an astronomy education and celestial-model application for HarmonyOS watches. We follow data-minimisation principles: models, motion tracking, and visual effects are processed locally on the device. The app does not create user accounts and does not collect, sell, or upload personal information.

Permissions and sensors

  • Rotation vector and gyroscope: rotate celestial bodies and the star background in Follow mode. Samples are processed only while the model page is open and are neither uploaded nor retained.
  • Pressure sensor for supported Huawei windows: controls the lighting shadow on compatible devices. Ordinary touchscreen pressure is not used for this feature.
  • Vibration: provides haptic feedback for crown paging, zooming, boundaries, and long-press confirmation.
  • Network and network state: are used only when you explicitly open this policy, check for updates, or use a clearly identified online feature.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, messages, call logs, or health-data permissions.

How data is handled

  • Sensor samples exist briefly in memory and stop being processed when the corresponding page closes.
  • Sensitivity, browsing style, scale, and display preferences are stored only in the app's private local directory and contain no identity information.
  • Preferences remain until you reset them, clear app data, or uninstall the app.
  • The current version contains no advertising, behavioural analytics, account-login, or personalised-recommendation SDKs and does not sell, rent, or share personal information.
  • When you open this page, the hosting provider may receive an IP address and basic request information as part of a normal web connection. The developer does not use this information to build user profiles.

Third-party services

The app contains no advertising, analytics, or social-sharing services. This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose network-log processing is governed by GitHub's own policies. Licences and credits for astronomy images, textures, and models do not give those asset providers access to user data.

Your rights

You can manage permissions in system settings, adjust or reset preferences in the app, and delete local preferences by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. For access, correction, deletion, restriction, withdrawal of consent, or questions about this policy, contact us using the email below. We will respond to reasonable requests within 15 working days.

Children

The app is intended for general astronomy education. It does not request age or identity information and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Children should use the app with guidance from a parent or guardian.

Security and updates

We reduce risk through application sandboxing, minimal permissions, and local processing. If features, permissions, or processing practices materially change, we will update the date on this page and provide prominent notice or renewed consent where required by law.
